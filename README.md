## 👋 About me

I'm an **industrial engineer who fell in love with data**. Today I work as a **Mid Data Scientist in AI at [NielsenIQ](https://nielseniq.com/)**; before that I was the lead data scientist for **Avianca**, Colombia's largest airline, at BRM.

Everything I build answers one question: **how do we turn messy data into better decisions?** My answer mixes **machine learning** with **operations research** (the subject of my engineering thesis), and I like to ship the result as something people use every day: a dashboard, an API, an automation.
